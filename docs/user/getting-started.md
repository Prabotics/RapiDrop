# Getting Started with RapiDrop

RapiDrop lets you seamlessly share files and synchronize your clipboard between your own devices over your local Wi-Fi network.

## Supported Platforms

* **macOS**: macOS 14+ (Apple Silicon & Intel)
* **Android**: Android 10+ (API 29+)
* **Windows**: Windows 10 & 11 (64-bit)

## How It Works

1. **Connect to the same Wi-Fi network**: RapiDrop communicates directly over your local network. No internet access or cloud servers are required.
2. **Open RapiDrop on both devices**: Nearby devices will automatically appear on the Radar discovery screen.
3. **Pair your devices once**: Tap your peer device on the radar. The other device will see an invitation with a matching 6-digit confirmation code. Tap **Accept** to establish a secure, trusted pairing.
4. **Start sharing**: Copy text or images on one device and paste on another, or drag and drop files to send them instantly.

## Quick Installation

### macOS
1. Download `RapiDrop.dmg` or build from source using Swift.
2. Drag `RapiDrop.app` into your Applications folder.
3. Launch RapiDrop. An icon appears in your menu bar.

### Android
1. Install the RapiDrop APK.
2. Grant Notification and Battery Optimization permissions when prompted so RapiDrop can sync reliably in the background.
3. (Optional) Add the **RapiDrop Quick Settings Tile** to your notification shade for one-tap clipboard sharing.

### Windows
1. Download and run `RapiDrop.exe`.
2. RapiDrop runs in your system tray. Click the tray icon to open the flyout window.
