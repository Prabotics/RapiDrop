RapiDrop syncs your clipboard and files directly between your Android, macOS, and Windows devices — no cloud, no accounts, no sign-up.

Everything stays on your local Wi-Fi network. Nothing leaves your home or office.

Features:

• Clipboard Sync — Copy on one device, paste on another. Text, links, and images sync instantly.
• File Transfer — Send files of any size, direct device-to-device. No upload limits.
• End-to-End Encrypted — Every transfer is encrypted and verified. No server ever touches your data.
• Zero Tracking — No accounts, no analytics, no external servers. Works fully offline.
• Open Source — MIT licensed. Fully auditable at any time.
