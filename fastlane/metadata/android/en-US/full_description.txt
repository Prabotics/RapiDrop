RapiDrop is a decentralized, zero-cloud peer-to-peer clipboard and file synchronization app for Android, macOS, and Windows.

Features:
• Instant Clipboard Sync: Synchronize copied text, web links, and images between paired devices on your local network.
• Direct File Streaming: Send files of any size directly device-to-device with end-to-end encryption and streaming SHA-256 integrity verification.
• End-to-End Encryption: All communications use Curve25519 ECDH key exchange, numeric SAS verification, and per-packet AES-256-GCM encryption.
• Zero Cloud & Zero Telemetry: Operates strictly over your local Wi-Fi subnet using mDNS service discovery (_clipsync._tcp). No accounts, no external servers, and no trackers.
• Open Source: Fully auditable under the MIT license.
